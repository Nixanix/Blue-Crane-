NOT FOR PRODUCTION
==================

This folder is the earlier draft e-label pack. It is archived so the files are
not lost. Do not print these QR codes, publish these HTML files, or use these
nutrition figures on labels or on www.bluecrane.co.za.

The QR images here encode placeholder URLs on yourdomain.com, for example
https://yourdomain.com/elabel/bluecrane-shiraz-2022. The energy values in
README.txt are older estimates and differ from the print pack.

Production source of truth is at the repository root:

  qrcodes/
